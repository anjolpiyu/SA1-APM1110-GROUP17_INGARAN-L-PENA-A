SECTION II, PART 2: SHAPIRO-WILK NORMALITY TEST
Asset: Ethereum (ETH/USD)
Requested period: 2015-08-07 through 2026-09-15
Available period in filtered file: 2015-08-07 through 2026-06-30
Valid exact five-minute returns: 998244
Observations tested: 5000
W statistic: 0.6440712
p-value: < 2.2e-16
Significance level: 0.05
H0: The sampled log returns follow a normal distribution.
H1: The sampled log returns do not follow a normal distribution.
Decision: Reject the null hypothesis of normality
Interpretation: Because the p-value is below 0.05, reject the null hypothesis. The sampled Ethereum five-minute log returns show strong evidence of departure from a normal distribution.

Limitation: The available Kraken dataset ends on 2026-06-30, before the
requested end date of 2026-09-15. The analysis therefore applies only to
the available observation period and does not include the missing dates.
